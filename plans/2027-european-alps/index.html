European Alps: Grossglockner, Dolomites, Stelvio & the Swiss Passes

A seven-day loop from Munich: over the Grossglockner into Austria, through the Dolomites, up Stelvio twice, across the great Swiss passes and down to Lake Como, then home over the Timmelsjoch.

Arrive · Sat, Jun 12

Munich Airport to Erding

Fly into Munich and settle in Erding, home of the Erdinger wheat-beer brewery. Therme Erding, the largest thermal spa in the world, is a short ride away for jet-lagged legs. Evening: review the Day 1 route and check that the Grossglockner road is open.

Overnight

Hotel zum Erdinger Weißbräu

Erding, Germany

Prep · Sun, Jun 13

Bike pickup

A short ride from Erding to Allround Motorcycle Renting near the airport to collect the bikes, then a shakedown day. Munich, about 40 minutes away, has every moto gear shop for last-minute items.

Overnight

Hotel zum Erdinger Weißbräu

Erding, Germany

Day 1 · Mon, Jun 14

Munich Lienz

266 km · 165 mi · about 6 hours riding

South on the A8 to Chiemsee, the “Bavarian Sea”, then into Austria past Kufstein Fortress and on to Zell am See. The day’s highlight is the Grossglockner High Alpine Road: 36 hairpins, glaciers and the short side road up to Edelweißspitze at 2,571 m. Down through Heiligenblut and the Mölltal to Lienz, under the Lienz Dolomites.

By way of

  • Chiemsee
  • Kufstein
  • Zell am See
  • Grossglockner High Alpine Road
  • Edelweißspitze
  • Heiligenblut

Overnight

Grandhotel Lienz

Lienz, Austria

Day 2 · Tue, Jun 15

Lienz Schlanders

265 km · about 5.5 hours

Into Italy through the Puster Valley and down the old Alemagna road to Cortina d’Ampezzo. Then the Dolomites in a row: Passo Giau, Pordoi and Sella, before Val Gardena and the long run west through the apple orchards of the Venosta valley.

By way of

  • Toblach
  • Cortina d’Ampezzo
  • Passo Giau
  • Passo Pordoi
  • Passo Sella
  • Val Gardena
  • Venosta Valley

Overnight

Hotel Engel

Schluderns, Vinschgau, Italy

Day 3 · Wed, Jun 16

Schlanders Chur

255 km · about 5 hours

Up the 48 hairpins of the Stelvio Pass (2,757 m), then down the quieter Umbrail Pass into Switzerland and Val Müstair. Through the Munt la Schera tunnel to duty-free Livigno, over the Bernina Pass and the Flüela Pass, and down to Chur, Switzerland’s oldest town.

By way of

  • Stelvio Pass
  • Umbrail Pass
  • Santa Maria Val Müstair
  • Livigno
  • Bernina Pass
  • Flüela Pass

Along the way

  • The Smallest Whisky Bar on Earth — Santa Maria Val Müstair. Five stools and hundreds of whiskies, with a whisky museum next door.

Overnight

Hotel Chur

Chur, Switzerland

Day 4 · Thu, Jun 17

Chur Andermatt

About 215–230 km

West up the Rhine valley and over the Oberalp Pass to Andermatt. Then the Swiss “holy trinity” as a loop: the Furka Pass past the old Hotel Belvédère and the Rhône Glacier, the Grimsel Pass and its reservoirs, and the Susten Pass. Back to Andermatt through the Schöllenen Gorge and past the Devil’s Bridge.

By way of

  • Oberalp Pass
  • Furka Pass
  • Gletsch
  • Grimsel Pass
  • Innertkirchen
  • Susten Pass
  • Schöllenen Gorge

Overnight

Hotel 3 Könige & Post

Andermatt, Switzerland

Day 5 · Fri, Jun 18

Andermatt Menaggio

About 220–250 km

South over the Gotthard (the old pass road if the weather is good) into Ticino and down to Lake Maggiore. Along the western shore through Cannobio and Verbania, then east to Lake Como and its twisting lakeside road. An easier day, with time for lunch and gelato by the water.

By way of

  • Gotthard Pass
  • Airolo
  • Locarno
  • Lake Maggiore
  • Cannobio
  • Lake Como

Overnight

Lakeside hotel

Menaggio or Varenna, Lake Como, Italy

Day 6 · Sat, Jun 19

Menaggio Bolzano

About 240–260 km

North along Lake Como into the Valtellina wine valley to Bormio, then Stelvio again, this time from the east side. Down through the Venosta valley and the spa town of Merano to Bolzano, with the Dolomites ahead.

By way of

  • Colico
  • Valtellina
  • Sondrio
  • Bormio
  • Stelvio Pass
  • Merano

Overnight

Parkhotel Laurin

Bolzano, Italy

Day 7 · Sun, Jun 20

Bolzano Munich

360 km · about 6.5 hours

Up the Passeier valley and over the Timmelsjoch High Alpine Road into Austria’s Ötztal, along the Inn valley and over the Möserer Pass into Bavaria. Back past Garmisch to Munich to return the bikes.

By way of

  • Passeier Valley
  • Timmelsjoch
  • Ötztal
  • Inn Valley
  • Möserer Pass
  • Garmisch-Partenkirchen

Overnight

Holiday Inn Express Munich Airport

Munich, Germany

Depart · Tue, Jun 22

Fly home from Munich
